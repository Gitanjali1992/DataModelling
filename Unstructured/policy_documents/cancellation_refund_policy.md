# FastBite — Order Cancellation & Refund Policy

**Document type:** Customer-facing policy
**Last updated:** 2026-01-01

## Standard Cancellations
Orders cancelled before the restaurant confirms preparation receive a full refund to the original payment method within 3–5 business days. Orders cancelled after the restaurant has started preparing the food receive a 50% refund. Orders cancelled after a courier has been assigned for pickup are non-refundable.

## Restaurant-Side Cancellations
If a restaurant cancels an order after confirming it (out of stock, closed kitchen, etc.), the customer receives a full refund plus a $5 FastBite credit, regardless of how far along the order was.

## Late Delivery Refunds
Orders delivered more than 30 minutes past the estimated delivery time shown at checkout are eligible for a partial refund of the delivery fee. Orders delivered more than 60 minutes late are eligible for a full refund of the delivery fee plus a 10% credit on the food total.

## Missing or Incorrect Items
Missing items are refunded at the item's listed price once confirmed. Incorrect items (wrong dish delivered) are refunded in full for the affected item, and the customer keeps whatever was delivered.

## Quality Issues
Orders arriving cold, spilled, or otherwise not fit to eat are eligible for a full refund or a replacement order at no charge, at the customer's choice, provided the issue is reported within 24 hours of delivery.

## How to Request a Refund
Refund requests should be submitted through Support with the order number and reason for the request. Most refund decisions are communicated within 2 business days of the request.
