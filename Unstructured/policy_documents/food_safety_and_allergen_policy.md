# FastBite — Food Safety & Allergen Policy

**Document type:** Restaurant-partner and customer-facing policy
**Last updated:** 2026-01-01

## Allergen Labeling
Restaurant partners must flag the eight major allergens (peanuts, tree nuts, dairy, egg, wheat, soy, fish, shellfish) on every dish listing before it can go live on FastBite. Dishes missing allergen data are hidden from search until corrected.

## Temperature Standards
Hot food must leave the restaurant at or above 60°C (140°F); cold or chilled items at or below 5°C (41°F). Couriers using insulated bags are expected to maintain these ranges for deliveries under 30 minutes; anything longer requires restaurant-provided extra insulation.

## Cross-Contamination Requests
Customers may flag a "severe allergy" note at checkout. Restaurants receiving such an order must confirm preparation in dedicated equipment where possible, or cancel the item with no penalty if that can't be guaranteed.

## Reporting a Food Safety Issue
Customers reporting suspected foodborne illness are routed to a dedicated Trust & Safety queue, separate from standard quality refunds, and the restaurant is notified within 1 hour for internal review.

## Restaurant Compliance
Restaurants with three or more substantiated food safety complaints in a rolling 90-day period are temporarily suspended from new orders pending a compliance review. Repeat violations result in permanent removal from the platform.

## Recall Notices
If a restaurant issues a recall on an ingredient or dish, FastBite pauses new orders for the affected dish immediately and notifies any customer who ordered it in the prior 7 days.
