# FastBite — Order Support & Missing Items Guide

**Document type:** Customer-facing support guide
**Last updated:** 2026-01-01

## My Order Never Arrived
Check the live tracking map first — most "missing" orders are a courier delayed in traffic or a wrong drop-off location. If tracking shows Delivered but nothing arrived, contact Support immediately with the order number; these cases are prioritized and typically resolved within 30 minutes.

## An Item Is Missing
Check your receipt in the app against what arrived. Missing items are refunded automatically once confirmed through Support chat — no need to return anything.

## I Got the Wrong Order
Do not eat food from a bag that doesn't match your order confirmation, especially if you have allergies. Report it through Support right away; you'll receive a full refund and the option to reorder at no extra delivery charge.

## The Food Arrived Cold or Damaged
Report this within 24 hours with a description (a photo helps but isn't required). Depending on the issue, you can choose a refund or a free replacement order.

## I Can't Reach My Courier
Use the in-app chat or call feature first. If there's no response within 10 minutes and the delivery window has passed, contact Support directly and reference the order number.

## Order Stuck in "Preparing"
If a restaurant hasn't updated an order's status for more than 20 minutes past the estimate, Support can check directly with the restaurant or offer to cancel with a full refund.

## Escalating a Support Case
If a chat resolution doesn't resolve the issue, ask to escalate to a senior support agent, referencing your case number. Escalated cases are reviewed within 4 hours during business hours.
