# FastBite — Courier Conduct & Delivery Standards

**Document type:** Internal / courier-facing policy
**Last updated:** 2026-01-01

## Pickup Standards
Couriers must arrive at the restaurant within 5 minutes of the "ready for pickup" notification. Orders left unclaimed at the restaurant for more than 15 minutes are reassigned to the next available courier.

## Delivery Window
Couriers are expected to deliver within the estimated window shown to the customer at checkout. Delays beyond 10 minutes should be flagged to the customer through the in-app message, not left silent.

## Handling Food
Hot and cold items must be bagged separately when the restaurant provides separate packaging. Insulated bags are mandatory for all deliveries over 15 minutes; couriers found delivering without one after a first warning are suspended for one week.

## Contact at the Door
Couriers must attempt customer contact (knock, doorbell, or call) before marking an order as delivered without contact. "Leave at door" instructions still require a photo confirmation.

## Vehicle & Region Rules
Bike and scooter couriers are restricted to regions with dedicated delivery lanes or low-traffic zones; car couriers cover the wider regional radius. Vehicle type on file must match the vehicle actually used for a delivery.

## Conduct Standards
Rudeness, unsafe driving, or unauthorized detours reported through customer feedback are reviewed within 48 hours. Three substantiated complaints in a rolling 90-day window trigger a formal review of the courier's account.

## Escalation
Couriers experiencing unsafe pickup or delivery conditions (aggressive customers, unsafe premises) should cancel the delivery through the app and contact Courier Support immediately rather than resolving it themselves.
