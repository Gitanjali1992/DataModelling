# FastBite — Restaurant Partner Agreement Terms

**Document type:** Partner-facing agreement summary
**Last updated:** 2026-01-01

## Commission Structure
FastBite charges a 22% commission on the food subtotal of each delivered order placed through the platform. Delivery fees and tips are passed through to FastBite's courier network and are not part of the commission base.

## Menu & Pricing
Restaurants control their own menu items, categories, and prices, and may update them at any time through the Partner Portal. Prices shown to customers must match in-restaurant prices within 5%.

## Order Acceptance Time
Restaurants must accept or reject an incoming order within 3 minutes of receipt. Orders not acted on within that window are automatically cancelled and the restaurant is flagged for review after three occurrences in a week.

## Category Listings
New menu categories (e.g. adding Vegan or Seafood to an existing menu) require restaurants to confirm dish-level allergen and prep-time data before the category goes live in search and filters.

## Preparation Time Accuracy
Restaurants set an estimated prep time per dish, used to calculate the courier pickup window shown to customers. Consistently inaccurate prep times (more than 10 minutes off, repeatedly) trigger a review and may affect search ranking.

## Termination
Either party may terminate the partnership with 30 days' written notice. FastBite may suspend a restaurant immediately for repeated food safety violations, fraud, or sustained customer complaint patterns, per the Food Safety & Allergen Policy.

## Payouts
Restaurant payouts are processed weekly, net of commission and any applicable promo-code reimbursements, to the bank account on file in the Partner Portal.
