# FastBite — Loyalty Program FAQ (FastBite Points)

**Document type:** Customer-facing FAQ
**Last updated:** 2026-01-01

## How do I earn points?
You earn 1 FastBite Point per $1 spent on food (delivery fees and tips don't count). Points are credited once an order is marked Delivered, not at checkout.

## What are points worth?
100 points = $1 in FastBite credit, redeemable at checkout on any order over $10. Points don't expire as long as your account has at least one order every 12 months.

## Do cancelled or refunded orders earn points?
No. If an order is cancelled or fully refunded after points were credited, the points are deducted from your balance on the next login.

## Are there bonus point events?
Yes. Restaurants can opt into "double points" windows during off-peak hours, shown directly on the restaurant's page when active. Promo codes and points can be combined on the same order.

## Is there a loyalty tier system?
Customers who place 15 or more orders in a rolling 90-day window are automatically upgraded to FastBite Plus, which includes free delivery on orders over $20 and priority support routing.

## How do I check my balance?
Your points balance is shown on the account home screen and in the receipt email after every delivered order.

## Can points be transferred or gifted?
No, points are tied to the account that earned them and cannot be transferred, sold, or combined across accounts.
